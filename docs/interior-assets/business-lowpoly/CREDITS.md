# Business low-poly library

All new models in this folder are distributed under CC0 1.0. Per-model source URLs,
authors, upstream file checksums, output checksums, triangle counts, dimensions and
validation results are in `../manifest.json`. Models were normalized to a bottom-center
metre pivot with representative game dimensions; these are not surveyed object sizes.

- **Kay Lousberg / KayKit**, [Restaurant Bits](https://kaylousberg.itch.io/restaurant-bits),
  [official source repository](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Restaurant-Bits-1.0),
  revision `153c8a7535b48237854cb54ff6890679f8c574d1`. Free CC0 edition only.
- **Kenney**, [Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit). Bundled license:
  Creative Commons Zero, CC0; free personal, educational and commercial use.
  Sofa-derived banquette seats retain their author/model identities; they are not
  claimed to be bespoke restaurant booth scans.
- **Grab3D**, [Cozy Hospital & Medical Kit](https://grab3d.com/kits/cozy-hospital-medical/).
  Each source asset page identifies CC0, personal/commercial use and direct downloads.
  The light edition is used where available, then simplified within the recorded budget.

[CC0 1.0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No attribution is required by CC0; source credit is retained for provenance. Imported
models are limited to 8,500 triangles and 512-pixel textures, and pass glTF validation
without errors. Geometric simplification and rescaling were performed for Kerbside.
