How

  1. Each photograph squared up from its own vanishing points (smc.facades.photo_rectify).
  2. The front panel cut out and given only its width and height in metres, read from the drawing's scale bar -- the priors the footprint and the lidar give in the city.
  3. The pipeline unchanged: segmentation, detector, extract_front.
  4. Openings marked on the drawing and matched to the predicted ones (centres within max(1.5 m, the opening's size), overlap IoU >= 0.1).

Coverage

3 of the 12 houses could be scored (4 front panels, 15 drawn openings): a house needs a photograph square enough to the front and that front's measured elevation. Not scored:

  • John Muir House, Martinez (HABS CA-1890) — measured drawings only: no photograph of the house in the mirror
  • Camp Reynolds Building 46 (HABS CA-1841-C) — photographs, but no elevation sheet in the mirror (details only)
  • Camp Reynolds Buildings 44-45 (HABS CA-1841-B) — photographs, but no elevation sheet in the mirror (details only)
  • Camp Reynolds Buildings 48-49 (HABS CA-1841-D) — a square-on photograph, but only floor plans and details in the mirror
  • Vallejo Adobe, Petaluma (HABS CA-38-1) — the one exterior photograph is distant and oblique, across a field
  • Vicente Martinez Adobe (HABS CA-1913) — one drawing sheet, no photograph
  • Pena Adobe, Vacaville (HABS CA-1198) — the 1930s photograph shows the wall mid-restoration; its left end could not be tied to the drawing
  • Band Master's Quarters, Building 55 (HABS CA-1841-E) — photographed through trees; plan sheet only
  • Rodoni Property, El Cerrito (HALS CA-143) — not in the mirror; the Library of Congress refused automated downloads

Historic American Buildings Survey (Library of Congress), public domain; scans from the Wikimedia Commons mirror -- the Library of Congress refused automated downloads on the day. Photographs and drawings were deleted after scoring; only these numbers are kept. Data: habs-2026-10-12.json.
