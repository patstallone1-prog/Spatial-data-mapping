The camera opens the wide lens and waits for a position. Both have to be there before the shutter unlocks — a frame with no fix cannot be placed on the map afterwards, and a frame off the narrow lens matches its neighbours less than half as often.

Bring in frames from the glasses app, the camera roll, or a memory card. They are copied into this app's own storage and scored on arrival; position and capture time are read from each file. Nothing is uploaded until the cull has run and you have said so.

What is waiting

Frames stay on this device until the nightly run keeps them, and are erased once the batch is written. Nothing leaves automatically.

The nightly run

Reviews the day, drops what carries no information, compresses what survives, writes one batch, then erases the local copies. Runs by itself at 2am when the app is open; you can also run it now.

What gets dropped, and why

  • PersonAnyone is the subject. Deleted before anything is compressed or written. Never leaves the device.
  • BlurredJudged against the day, not a fixed number — a brick wall and an overcast sky score differently at identical focus.
  • DuplicateNear-identical to a sharper frame. Standing still produces dozens; they share a viewpoint and a GPS error, so they are not corroboration.
  • ExposureToo dark, or blown out. Nothing recoverable either way.
  • SurplusPast a quota for one place. The budget is better spent where there is no coverage at all.
