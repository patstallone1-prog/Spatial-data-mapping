The city's birds are built from these models, each licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/):

  "Seagull" by Poly by Google -- https://poly.pizza/m/0WRzrtCIIRp
  "Flying seagull" by Poly by Google -- https://poly.pizza/m/6Tpj_vcWP3f
  "Dove" by Poly by Google -- https://poly.pizza/m/2jmH3trzPFf
  "Crow" by Poly by Google -- https://poly.pizza/m/1MIvWQ5Q3R9
  "Sparrow" by Poly by Google -- https://poly.pizza/m/3rTjKefT184
  "Flying Raven" by Google Data Arts Team -- https://poly.pizza/m/p4puYPVor6

Changes: textures baked to vertex colours, re-oriented and scaled to each species, wings cut at
the shoulder and levelled for flapping, decimated, and recoloured (the dove as a rock pigeon, the
seagull as a western gull, the flying seagull as a pigeon in flight, the flying raven as a
sparrow in flight). See birds.json and tools/blender/build_city_birds.py.
