# Fuel, EV and chain reference additions — 2026-10-30

## chargepoint-layout

[Express 250 Civil and Mechanical Design](https://docs.chargepoint.com/cpdocs-sec/content/3-dc/express-250/sdg/civil-mechanical-design.htm) — ChargePoint; manufacturer guide; Product-specific, North America.

- Express 250 enclosure height 2.230 m and width 0.71 m are documented product dimensions.
- Cable reach radius is 3.76 m for this product. Cable reach and service clearance are distinct.
- Bollards should not interfere with holsters or cables; the guide states no bollard within 457 mm on-center.

## tesla-parking

[Supercharging Other EVs](https://www.tesla.com/support/charging/supercharging-other-evs) — Tesla; manufacturer guide; North America.

- Vehicle port positions vary. Short-cable stations can require an adjacent stall; avoid diagonal parking.
- V4 posts have longer cables than earlier posts. A brand name alone does not establish installed hardware generation.
