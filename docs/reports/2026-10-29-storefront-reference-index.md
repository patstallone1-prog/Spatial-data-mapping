# Storefront reference index — 2026-10-29

This is a qualitative reference library. Historical proposals and prototype images are not asserted as present-day as-built conditions. Numeric layout ranges are engine priors, not measurements from these sources.

43 sources · 25 category profiles · 13 chain identity families

## Sources

### sf-formula

[Formula Retail Storefront Design](https://sfplanning.org/sites/default/files/documents/publications/Guide_FormulaRetail_Commission.pdf) — SF Planning; design guideline; San Francisco.

Storefront Components and Formula Retail Storefront Design.

- Storefront bays distinguish piers, bulkheads, glazing, transoms and entrances.
- Retain historic components when inserting chain identity; a brand does not imply a replacement building shell.

### sf-historic

[Downtown Historic Design Standards](https://sfplanning.org/sites/default/files/documents/publications/Downtown_Historic_Design_Standards.pdf) — SF Planning; design guideline; San Francisco.

Awnings, Canopies, and Marquees.

- Awnings fit between piers and should leave transoms visible.
- Sloped awnings and signs belong to individual storefront bays, not across unrelated tenants.

### sf-transparency

[Standards for Storefront Transparency](https://sfplanning.org/resource/standards-storefront-transparency) — SF Planning; design guideline; San Francisco.

- Front display glazing remains visually open rather than fully covered by invented posters.
- The guidance concerns storefront visibility, not a measurement of any existing store.

### sf-calle24

[Calle 24 Special Area Design Guidelines](https://sfplanning.org/sites/default/files/documents/citywide/calle-24-special-area-design-guidelines/calle_24_design_guidelines.pdf) — SF Planning; design guideline; San Francisco Mission.

- Neighborhood signs include awnings, painted signs and multilingual business identity.
- Laundromat examples combine an overhead identity band and limited window lettering.

### starbucks-identity

[Starbucks Creative Expression: Logos and Color](https://creative.starbucks.com/?edition=starter) — Starbucks; brand guideline; Global.

- The Siren and wordmark are distinct brand elements.
- Greens, neutrals and a restrained type system provide recognizable identity.
- This reference is a visual identity guide, not a universal exterior elevation.

### starbucks-sf

[Starbucks Bay Area projects](https://www.msasf.com/rt-starbucks) — MSA architecture + design; built project; San Francisco Chestnut, Mission & Main, Market.

Mission & Main exterior photograph; visually inspected.

- Mission & Main photograph: tall black-framed glazing with transom, a deep soffit and gold-toned illuminated lettering.
- Reserve locations and standard coffeehouses have different expressions.
- Existing structure and local conditions drive individual site detailing.

### starbucks-local

[Starbucks Store Design](https://en.starbucks.vn/coffeehouse/store-design/) — Starbucks; brand guideline; Global.

- Designs respond to neighborhood character and use local materials.
- Do not assert that every Starbucks has the same facade.

### subway-identity

[Subway visual identity](https://turnerduckworth.com/work/subway) — Turner Duckworth; brand designer; Global.

- Arrow-ended logotype and yellow/green identity were developed as a coordinated system.
- Signage is part of a wider environmental identity; the type is custom.

### subway-2024

[Subway Unveils New Global Restaurant Design](https://newsroom.subway.com/2024-11-12-Subway-Unveils-New-Global-Restaurant-Design) — Subway; prototype; Global.

Exterior media item 19669; visually inspected, a design rendering not a built-site survey.

- Published exterior concept has a green fascia with yellow/white dimensional wordmark, white cladding and a glass front with green-framed entry.
- Front glazing and solid side wall have different functions.
- Fresh Forward 2.0 rollout does not establish the remodel status of a particular Bay Area store.

### subway-fabrication

[Subway Fresh Forward signage](https://www.vgsonline.com/portfolio/subway) — Visual Graphic Systems; fabricator; Global.

- Signage was prototyped and value-engineered as a repeatable franchise system.

### safeway-ssf

[Safeway Shopping Center 180 El Camino Real](https://ci-ssf-ca.legistar.com/LegislationDetail.aspx?FullText=1&GUID=C91B96B4-717A-4E1E-A8E9-1DABF1940327&ID=3269780) — City of South San Francisco; historic approved proposal; South San Francisco.

Approved Project Plans, cover rendering visually inspected; October 2017 approval.

- Customer-facing elevation combines broad glass storefront bays with brick and plaster framing.
- Loading and back-of-house side/rear walls are different from the public front.
- Approved plan shows dark lettering and a red S medallion; it does not prove the present-day as-built condition.

### safeway-spec

[Safeway sign specifications, Oakley](https://d3n9y02raazwpg.cloudfront.net/oakley/da030dd0-c92e-47cd-8b87-dadfc12fbed1-cd570418-cb00-4b7a-9e50-80f71e682aee-1742414974.pdf) — City of Oakley planning packet; sign specification; Oakley.

SAFEWAY SIGN SPECIFICATIONS; indexed text, dimensions not transcribed.

- Sign specification calls for LED channel letters/logo and maintaining letter height-to-width ratios.
- Sign size is site dependent, not a fixed fraction of every Safeway.

### wholefoods-oakland

[Whole Foods Market Oakland](https://lowneyarch.com/work/whole-foods-market-oakland/) — Lowney Architecture; built project; Oakland.

- Historic facade is retained in an adaptive reuse.
- A steel and glass connection links the store with rooftop and street-level parking.
- This facade cannot be copied as a standard suburban Whole Foods shell.

### wholefoods-rollout

[Whole Foods Market national program](https://www.brrarch.com/portfolio/grocery-stores/whole-foods-market/) — BRR Architecture; architect program; United States including California.

- Exterior design is coordinated with developers and location-specific goals.
- Neighborhood context varies across the chain.

### traderjoes-program

[Trader Joes market program](https://jhai-architect.com/index.php/projects/commercial/retail-markets/trader-joes-company) — JHAI; architect program; California and other markets.

- Work covers many existing and new market shells, including exterior elevations.
- Practical adaptation and city requirements differ by location.

### traderjoes-signs

[Trader Joes Signage](https://www.rogerchandesign.com/work/traderjoes) — Roger Chan Design; brand designer; United States.

- Brand lettering coexists with local illustrated and hand-made sign treatments.
- Do not replace every location with an identical architectural shell.

### peets-chestnut

[Peets 2078–2080 Chestnut Street](https://sfplanning.s3.amazonaws.com/default/files/meetingarchive/planning_dept/commissions.sfplanning.org/cpcpackets/2013.0245C.pdf) — SF Planning / Crome Architecture; historic approved proposal; San Francisco Marina.

2013 packet p36, sheet A3.1; visually inspected.

- Proposed elevation retains a historic entry, tile base and upper decorative facade.
- New glazing aligns with existing glazing; signs avoid historic tile.
- Street-level transparency is documented as 72.6 percent for this proposal, not a chain-wide ratio.

### peets-prototype

[Peets Coffee prototype program](https://chipman.design/work2/peets-coffee) — Chipman Design Architecture; architect program; Los Gatos and rollout markets.

- Prototype kit balances wood, stone, tile and metal.
- Drive-through and walk-in formats differ and retain site-specific adaptation.

### philz-program

[Designing the Community Coffee House](https://www.dewberry.com/projects/philz-coffee) — Dewberry; architect program; California including Lafayette.

- A large finish catalogue is used to keep individual stores distinct.
- Local graphics and site-specific colors sit within a consistent coffeehouse identity.

### seven-identity

[7-Eleven Brand Identity](https://design.deutsch.com/7-Eleven) — Deutsch Design; brand designer; United States.

Store.jpg on the original designer portfolio; visually inspected.

- Three colored stripes remain an important identity device.
- The identity update is not evidence for a store-specific remodel.
- The published storefront example has orange above green above red, interrupted by centered identity on a white fascia over brick and glass.

### seven-media

[7-Eleven Media Kit](https://corp.7-eleven.com/corp/media-kit) — 7-Eleven; brand media; United States.

- Official brand graphics and store imagery provide reference for logo/stripe identity.

### chipotle-program

[Chipotle Eco Concept Pilot](https://ia.design/projects/chipotle) — IA Interior Architects; prototype; United States.

- Wood and metal form a restrained repeatable kit of parts.
- Pickup-focused and dine-in flows can use different shells; atmosphere is not excessive signage.

### chipotle-sites

[Chipotle rollout projects](https://www.wickarch.com/hospitality-portfolio/chipotle-restaurant-architecture) — Wick Architecture + Design; architect program; California.

- Prototype components are tailored to each site rather than changing the brand identity.

### chase-historic

[JPMorgan Chase Savannah](https://bdgllp.com/portfolio/jpmorgan-chase-savannah/) — BDG Architects; built project; Savannah, out-of-region comparison.

- Historic storefront and entry details are retained rather than replaced with a generic bank box.

### bank-signs

[Bank and Financial Institution Signage](https://sweetsign.com/industry/banking-and-financial-institution-signage) — Sweet Sign Systems; fabricator; United States, out-of-region comparison.

- Channel letters, logos, wayfinding and ATM identity are distinct sign components.

### cvs-portola

[CVS 701 Portola Drive elevations](https://commissions.sfplanning.org/cpcpackets/2011.0084C.pdf) — SF Planning; historic approved proposal; San Francisco.

CVS 1983, sheet A-3; indexed text, historical proposal.

- The plan differentiates clear customer glazing from stockroom walls.
- A metal canopy and cornice articulate a corner retail facade.

### walgreens-variation

[Walgreens Los Angeles elevations](https://cityclerk.lacity.org/onlinedocs/2011/11-0191_app_2-3-11.pdf) — City of Los Angeles; historic approved proposal; Los Angeles, out-of-region comparison.

- Pharmacy identity and window arrangements vary by elevation and existing structure.
- Legacy operating-hour and photo-service claims are not reused as current advertisements.

### anchor-oyster

[Anchor Oyster Bar Legacy Business](https://www.sfheritage.org/news/legacy-business-spotlight-anchor-oyster-bar-babylon-burning-and-veritable-vegetable/) — SF Heritage; original documentation; San Francisco Castro.

- The small restaurant has a broad plate-glass window and a blue awning.
- A narrow business can be recognizable without a large plastic fascia.

### bix

[Bix Restaurant](https://www.sfheritage.org/heritage-in-the-neighborhoods/bix-restaurant/) — SF Heritage; original documentation; San Francisco Jackson Square.

- A discreet alley entrance and a characteristic neon sign identify an upscale restaurant.
- High price does not automatically imply huge windows or oversized signage.

### gino-carlo

[Gino and Carlo](https://www.sfheritage.org/events/heritage-happy-hour-at-gino-and-carlo/) — SF Heritage; original documentation; San Francisco North Beach.

- A distinctive projecting neon blade sign identifies the bar.

### little-joes

[Little Joes Pizza](https://www.sfheritage.org/heritage-in-the-neighborhoods/legacy-business-spotlight-little-joes-pizza/) — SF Heritage; original documentation; San Francisco.

- Historic neon identity is a meaningful pizza-shop variant, not a universal requirement.

### great-wall

[Great Wall Hardware](https://www.sfheritage.org/news/legacy-business-spotlight-albert-chow-and-great-wall-hardware/) — SF Heritage; original documentation; San Francisco Parkside.

- Exterior includes bilingual business identification and a practical overhead sign.

### stout-books

[William Stout Architectural Books](https://stoutbooks.com/pages/about) — William Stout Architectural Books; merchant; San Francisco Jackson Square.

- A historic storefront provides the setting for a specialist bookstore.
- Display identity should leave the building architecture legible.

### credo

[Credo Beauty flagship](https://www.studio-bba.com/portfolio/credo-beauty) — Studio BBA; built project; San Francisco.

- Expansive operable glazing and a generous entry alcove connect the store to the street.
- Textured neutral plaster is punctuated by the brand color.

### florist

[Rossi and Rovetti Flower Shop](https://www.toddverwers.com/rossi-and-rovetti-flower-shop) — Todd Verwers Architects; built project; San Francisco.

- Contemporary flower display and flexible retail bins distinguish the flower-shop program.

### lamar

[La Mar](https://www.casscaldersmith.com/restaurants/la-mar) — Cass Calder Smith; built project; San Francisco waterfront.

- A glass canopy and large entrance identity are inserted within a historic pier facade.
- Restaurant context can change sign scale more than cuisine does.

### brucato

[Brucato Amaro](https://www.studio-bba.com/portfolio/brucato-amaro) — Studio BBA; built project; San Francisco Mission.

- Restaurant entry and shipping/receiving are adjacent but perform different roles.
- Tiled materials and neon/vertical identity are shown in project documentation.

### belmont-albany

[Belmont Village Albany](https://hkit.com/portfolio/belmont-village-albany/) — HKIT Architects; built project; Albany.

- Senior living is a multi-storey residential/institutional envelope with a distinct common entrance.
- Ground parking and upper residential windows must not be converted into storefront display glass.

### belmont-signs

[Belmont Village signage program](https://charlaine.com/projects/belmont-village-senior-living/) — Charlaine Architectural Signage; fabricator; California and Illinois.

- Identity letters, plaques, directories, addresses and wayfinding form a coordinated campus sign system.
- Advertising across every window is not the institutional program.

### medical-directory

[Forest Avenue Medical Center wayfinding](https://clearlinesigns.com/blog/posts/forest-avenue-medical-center-wayfinding-directory-signage) — Clear Line Signs; fabricator; San Jose.

- Multi-tenant medical uses need a shared directory and entrance/wayfinding identity.
- A directory is different from individual shop advertisements.

### office-guidance

[Office, Business Park, Institutional and Industrial Design](https://landuse.saccounty.gov/dg/ch5/) — Sacramento County; design guideline; Sacramento, out-of-region comparison.

- Signage is coordinated with architecture and landscaping.
- Office and institutional identity is organized at entrances rather than repeated storefront advertisements.

### cafe-practice

[Coffee shop design practice](https://www.studiobanaa.com/insights/how-to-start-a-coffee-shop-or-cafe-an-architects-perspective/) — Studio BANAA; architect practice; Bay Area.

- Brand and environmental graphics are separate from the shell.
- Existing chain spaces can be reused with different independent cafe identities.

### mels-history

[Mels Drive-In history and exterior documentation](https://melsdrive-in.com/history/) — Mels Drive-In; merchant; San Francisco.

Historic restaurant photographs and neon identity.

- Merchant photographs document the drive-in diner tradition, broad customer glazing and neon identity.
- Historic and current-format examples are not a universal contemporary diner elevation.
