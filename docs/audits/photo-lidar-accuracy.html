Photo / lidar accuracy audit

5 cm real-world placement is not verified. These tests do not replace the canonical world. Failed cases and missing references remain in the success-rate denominator. Raw photographs and candidate geometry are not published.

The real tests use calibrated PandaSet photographs, ALIKED/LightGlue, subpixel matching and separate fitting/evaluation lidar sweeps. This measures local sensor agreement, not absolute survey accuracy. Full camera-orientation refinement remains experimental.

Current development result: 7.95 cm median, 23.03 cm P90 over eight finite checkpoints in one of eight attempted cases. This is accepted as the current prototype result, not a map-wide accuracy claim. The untouched full-pose subset has 11.23 cm median and 228.19 cm P90; it does not demonstrate reliable generalization. No additional tuning was performed to chase 5 cm.

How to read the results

Success requires every planned checkpoint in a case to be within 5 cm, with complete reference support. Finite-point median/P90 describe only available predictions; they must not be mistaken for all-attempt accuracy. Unbounded all-attempt error means unsupported or failed cases remain.

The synthetic test is an analytic regression fixture, not a reconstruction of real streets. The untouched test uses different sequences, cameras and frames from development.

The downloaded 2023 USGS SF control cell has 74,734 returns. Its published horizontal accuracy is 12 cm at 95% confidence, so it cannot alone certify 5 cm absolute positioning. Official source metadata.
